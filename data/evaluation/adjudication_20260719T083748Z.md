  botanically a drupe, not a tree nut, though FDA ingredient-labeling

  statements — a labeling convention, not a botanical or (for most
  coconut-tolerant tree-nut-allergic individuals) a clinical fact. The
  judge itself never matched on "coconut" or "peanut" for this case
  (only on `almond flour` vs. bare `flour`), so this caveat does not
  change the verdict — it forecloses a plausible-looking but incorrect
  alternate TRUE argument a future reviewer might otherwise construct
  from this serve's ingredient list.
- Rule: bare `flour` in both serves is ordinary wheat flour (cookie
  dough); "almond" appears nowhere in either ingredient list. Same
  head-noun artifact class as `derivative_018`/`derivative_027`.
