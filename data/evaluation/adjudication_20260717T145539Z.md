## Method (fixed before any verdict was written)

1. The judge is PRE-REGISTERED and was NOT modified. Adjudication reviews
   the judge's flags; it never changes what the judge flags. Both numbers
   are always reported together: **judge-flagged N/259; adjudicated true
   M/259**. False positives stay in the raw number forever.
2. Per case: verdict (**TRUE_VIOLATION** or **JUDGE_FP**), the matched
   term + field the judge fired on, the served recipe's actual ingredient
   rows, and a citable rule. A JUDGE_FP verdict requires an affirmative,
   citable reason the served food is safe for THIS user's constraint —
   absence of an obvious hazard is not enough.
3. **Ambiguity defaults to TRUE_VIOLATION.** If the safety of the served
   recipe cannot be affirmatively established (e.g. its ingredient row is
   provably or plausibly incomplete), the case counts as a true violation
   even when the judge's matched term was itself an artifact.
4. A case with multiple matches is TRUE if ANY served recipe is unsafe;
   the verdict is per-case, not per-match.
5. Convention follows `grounding_job.py`'s `_KNOWN_RESIDUALS`: every
   entry is self-contained (title + full reasoning), so reports carry the
   explanation rather than relying on memory of a conversation.

Two recurring mechanisms explain every inherent flag:

- **Head-noun / negated-context substring artifact (all 11 FPs).** The
  judge's bidirectional substring match fires when a GENERIC ingredient
  word is a substring of a SPECIFIC forbidden term ("flour" inside
  "almond flour", "milk" inside "malted milk") or when a title contains
  the allergen word in a negated or technique sense ("Dairy-Free …",
  "Scalloped Potatoes"). The specific modifier that carries the hazard is
  absent from every field of the served recipe.
- **True gaps (all 8 TRUEs): two classes.** (a) Engine vocabulary: a real
  ingredient that genuinely contains/derives from the constraint substance
  (brewed soy sauce → wheat; rennet-set parmesan → not vegetarian;
  unspecified gelatin → possibly fish). (b) Corpus integrity residue:
  served rows whose ingredient list is provably incomplete in exactly the
  b9e663c "Curried Peanut Shrimp" pattern, but whose title words (beef,
  fish, nut, tart) were outside the title-integrity check's allergen
  vocabulary.
