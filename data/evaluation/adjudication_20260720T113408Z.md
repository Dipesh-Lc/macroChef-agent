## The 7 cases new to this round (all vs. commit `4a97b80~1`'s 16-case
## baseline in `adjudication_20260719T115815Z.md`) — all JUDGE_FP

All 7 share the same root cause: the JUDGE's own matcher
(`app/evaluation/benchmark/safety_judge.py`) uses a bidirectional substring
match (`match_rule: bidirectional_substring`) that lets a short, generic
ingredient word served by the recommendation graph (bare `"oil"`, bare
`"butter"`) reverse-match a longer, unrelated forbidden compound term (e.g.
bare `"oil"` "contains" `"arachis oil"` as a substring relationship, even
though the served recipe has no arachis/peanut content at all). This is a
known, already-documented artifact of the judge's OWN matcher — a different
component from `contains_allergen`/`app/services/constraint_engine.py`
(the production safety-filtering code, which is direction-aware and does
not have this defect after commit `4a97b80`'s reverse-arm removal). The
judge is never modified to close this gap (Honest Scope discipline); the
raw judge-flagged count stays published forever including these 7.
